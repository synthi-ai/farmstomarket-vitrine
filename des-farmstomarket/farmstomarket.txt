Farm'sToMarket From Farm to Shelf, Simplified.

Farm'sToMarket is the all-in-one platform that connects agricultural producers directly to retailers and buyers, removing the middlemen and putting more money back into the hands of those who grow our food.

Whether you are a farmer looking to sell your harvest, a retailer searching for fresh local produce, or a transporter ready to deliver goods, Farm'sToMarket brings everyone together in one seamless experience.

HOW IT WORKS

For Producers
List your crops, livestock, and farm products in just a few taps. Set your own prices with the help of real-time market data from our built-in Agricultural Stock Exchange. Manage incoming orders, track sales, and grow your business all from your phone.

For Retailers & Buyers
Discover fresh products directly from local farms. Browse listings, compare prices, view producer profiles, and place orders with confidence. No more guessing about quality or availability connect directly with the source.

For Transporters
Accept delivery requests, plan your routes, and complete deliveries with full tracking and documentation. Stay in touch with buyers and sellers through integrated messaging at every step of the journey.
a
KEY FEATURES

Direct Trade Buy and sell agricultural products without intermediaries. Better prices for farmers, fresher goods for buyers.

Secure Payments with AgroBank Our built-in escrow payment system protects every transaction. Buyers pay upfront, but funds are only released to the seller once delivery is confirmed. No fraud, no disputes.

Real-Time Delivery Tracking Follow your order from farm to doorstep with live GPS tracking, QR code confirmation, and step-by-step delivery updates.

Agricultural Stock Exchange Access real-time commodity prices and market trends to make smarter pricing and purchasing decisions.

Integrated Messaging Chat directly with producers, buyers, or transporters. Negotiate deals, ask questions, and coordinate deliveries without leaving the app.

AgroBank Financial Dashboard Manage your earnings, view transaction history, track escrow balances, and handle payments all in one place with zero transaction fees.

Smart Notifications Stay informed with real-time alerts for new orders, delivery updates, payment confirmations, and messages.

WHO IS IT FOR

- Farmers and agricultural producers who want to sell directly and earn more
- Retailers, restaurants, and food businesses looking for reliable local sourcing
- Transporters and delivery drivers seeking logistics opportunities
- Agricultural cooperatives looking to modernize their supply chain

WHY FARM'STOMARKET

Traditional agricultural supply chains are broken. Farmers earn too little, buyers pay too much, and everyone loses visibility once goods leave the farm. Farm'sToMarket fixes this by creating a transparent, secure, and efficient marketplace where every participant benefits.

- Farmers get fair prices and guaranteed payments
- Buyers get fresh products at competitive rates
- Transporters get reliable work with clear accountability
- Everyone gets real-time visibility from order to delivery

Join thousands of producers and buyers already transforming the way agricultural goods move from farm to market.

Farm'sToMarket Trade smarter. Deliver faster. Grow together.
